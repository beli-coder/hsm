Our Community

Carolina Friends School

Where Every Voice Matters

Carolina Friends School is a Quaker independent school in Durham, NC, serving students from preschool through 12th grade. Grounded in the values of integrity, community, equality, and peace, CFS nurtures curious minds and generous spirits.

Our arts program is central to our mission — giving every student a stage, a voice, and a community that cheers them on. The annual musical production is a beloved tradition that unites the entire CFS family.

Our Values

✦

Integrity

We act with honesty and hold ourselves to high standards in all we do — on stage and off.
