Our School

A Community Built on Friendship & Courage

Carolina Friends School is a Quaker independent school in Durham, NC. Founded in 1962, CFS provides a PreK–12 education grounded in the belief that there is “that of God” in every person — that each child holds inherent worth and potential.

Our performing arts program invites students to find their authentic voices on stage and in life. High School Musical is a celebration of everything this community stands for: courage, genuine connection, and the joy of showing up fully as yourself.

Our Values

✦

Community

Everyone belongs here. We build a school family that celebrates each person's unique gifts and encourages genuine connection across differences.

✦

Integrity

We speak truth and act with honesty — on stage, in class, and in all of life. Authenticity and trust are at the core of who we are.

✦

Creativity

The arts are essential to learning at CFS. We believe imagination and expression are as vital as any academic pursuit — not an add-on but a foundation.

✦

Equality

Every voice deserves to be heard. We are deeply committed to equity, inclusion, and genuine respect for every member of our community.
